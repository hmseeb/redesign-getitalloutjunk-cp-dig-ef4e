Junk Removal Guide

The L.A. Junk
Removal Guide

A short, practical guide to getting junk out of your home, garage, yard or storage unit — how pricing works, how to prep, and what to expect on pickup day.

Start with photos, not a phone tag marathon

The fastest way to price a junk removal job is to see it. Snap a few photos of the pile, the room, the garage or the storage unit and text them to (323) 381-6235. Wide shots help more than close-ups: we need to see volume, access and whether anything is especially heavy. From those photos we can send back an upfront price — no on-site estimate visit required, no waiting around.

Sort what goes in three piles

Before we arrive, it helps to loosely split things into keep, go and unsure. You don't need to move anything outside or carry a single box — we handle the lifting and loading. Knowing what stays is what matters, because it keeps the crew moving fast and keeps your price accurate.

Know what counts as junk removal

Furniture like sofas, tables, mattresses and dressers. Old appliances and bulky items. Garage clutter, boxes and old equipment. Full storage unit cleanouts. Yard debris, branches and outdoor junk. General household clutter and trash. Property cleanouts for homes, rentals and garages. And bulky pickups — the large items that won't fit in the bin and never get taken at the curb.

Clearing a garage or storage unit

Garages and storage units are the two jobs where people most often underestimate volume. Years of boxes stack deeper than they look. Photograph each wall and, if you can, tell us roughly how far back the pile goes. For a storage unit, note the unit size and the floor it's on — then we can clear the whole unit so you can hand back the key and stop paying the monthly bill.

Moving out or turning over a rental

For a move-out or rental turnover, the goal is empty and clean. Tell us your deadline when you reach out — same day availability is often open, and we schedule around you. We take it down to empty and leave the space clean and tidy so it's ready to show, hand over or list.

What to expect on pickup day

We arrive in the window we agreed on, confirm what's going, and load. You don't have to supervise every item — point at what goes and we take it from there. When the truck is loaded we sweep up and do a final walk through with you so there are no surprises, and the price is the price we quoted.

Questions to ask any junk removal company

Is the quote upfront or does it change on arrival? Who does the lifting? Will the space be left clean? How fast can you respond? Our answers: upfront quotes with no hidden fees, we do all the lifting and loading, we leave the area clean and tidy, and we get back to you quickly — many times same day.

The short version

Text photos or call (323) 381-6235, get an upfront price, and we haul it away. No complicated booking systems. No waiting around for estimates.
