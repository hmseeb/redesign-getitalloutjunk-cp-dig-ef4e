Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to send us. When you fill out a quote, contact or booking form on this site, that means your name, phone number, and — if you add them — your email address, ZIP code or area, the service you need, your preferred timing, and any notes you write. If you call or text us, we have your phone number and whatever you tell us.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your request: to send you an upfront price, to schedule your pickup, and to reach you about the job. That is it. We do not sell your information, and we do not rent or trade it.

Sharing

We share your information only with the people and services needed to do the work and run this site — for example, our own team, and the service that delivers this website's form submissions to us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Text messages and photos

If you text photos of your items for pricing, we use them to quote your job. Standard message and data rates from your carrier may apply.

Keeping your information

We keep quote requests and job records for as long as we need them to serve you and to keep our own business records.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you, or ask us to delete the information you sent us, at any time. Call or text (323) 381-6235 and we will take care of it.

Questions

Get It All Out LLC is based in Montebello, CA and serves the greater Los Angeles area. If you have a question about this policy, call or text (323) 381-6235.
